# UGC Playbook

> Read this in: reel-ugc (and ONLY when user explicitly asks for UGC)

---

## ⚠️ THE CARDINAL RULE — READ THIS BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE

**UGC in this system is NOT the UGC Claude has been trained on.**

Claude's default UGC patterns are:
- Talking-head "honest review" to camera
- Unboxing / first impression format
- "I tried this for 30 days" testimonial
- Lifestyle b-roll + voiceover
- Green-screen product overlay
- "POV: you just discovered..." caption style

**None of these are acceptable defaults here.** They are generic, overused, and immediately recognizable as UGC. The algorithm's originality scoring and savvy audiences both punish content that pattern-matches to known UGC templates.

---

## WHERE UGC COMES FROM IN THIS SYSTEM

**Source: The reference video. Only the reference video.**

The UGC angle is derived entirely from `01-reference-breakdown.md` — the edit style, pacing, energy, shot types, text design, emotional tone, and format of the specific reference the user provided for THIS video.

The question is never: *"What's a good UGC format for this product/topic?"*

The question is always: *"What would UGC look like if it was shot and edited in the exact same world as this reference video?"*

---

## HOW TO DERIVE THE UGC ANGLE FROM THE REFERENCE

### Step 1 — Identify the reference's DNA

From `01-reference-breakdown.md`, extract:

**Edit energy:** Is it frenetic (5+ cuts in 3s)? Slow burn? One long take? Rhythmic to a beat?
**POV:** First person walking? Overhead static? Close-up handheld? Over-shoulder?
**Sound design:** Music-driven? Dialogue-driven? ASMR-style? Ambient + text only?
**Text style:** Kinetic captions? One bold word at a time? No text, all visual?
**Emotional register:** Urgent / cautionary? Aspirational? Funny/absurd? Intimate/confessional?
**Hook mechanism:** What stopped the scroll in the reference — a visual, a sound, a word, a movement?

### Step 2 — Map the DNA onto the user's content

Now ask: if someone were creating UGC for THIS product/topic/person using the reference's DNA — what would it look like?

The reference's edit energy becomes the UGC's edit energy.
The reference's POV becomes the starting point for the UGC's shot selection.
The reference's hook mechanism becomes the UGC's hook mechanism.

### Step 3 — Find the point of departure

UGC has one job the reference doesn't: it must feel like it came from a real person, not a brand or creator. The point of departure is where the reference's style meets personal authenticity.

Ask: what is the one human moment in this style that feels earned, not performed?

That's the UGC angle.

## WHAT MAKES UGC WORK IN 2026

From `viral-principles.md` — the authenticity imperative:

> AI can generate polished content instantly → polished content is now cheap. Raw, human content stands out.

UGC's advantage is that it signals "real person" in a feed of produced content. But only if it actually feels real. Scripted-looking UGC is now actively harmful — audiences and the algorithm both recognize it.

**What makes UGC feel real:**
- Imperfect framing (slightly off-center, handheld wobble)
- Natural speech rhythm (hesitations, emphasis, not overly rehearsed)
- Genuine reaction shot — real expressions, not performed ones
- Environment that makes sense (not suspiciously clean/staged)
- The "I would actually say this" test — every line of dialogue must pass it

**What makes UGC feel fake (avoid):**
- Perfect lighting that looks like a ring light setup
- Overly smooth delivery with zero natural speech patterns
- Generic hooks ("I was today years old when I discovered...")
- Transitions or text effects that look like a brand produced it
- Anything that visually signals "this is an ad" before the viewer decides it's real

## UGC SIGNAL STRATEGY

UGC's primary goal is **DM shares** (Signal Tier 1 from `viral-principles.md`).

UGC works for shares because of **relational targeting** — the viewer watches and immediately thinks of a specific person: "I need to send this to [name]."

Design the UGC angle around: *Who is the specific person the viewer sends this to, and what emotion makes them hit send?*

The UGC scenario must be narrow and specific enough that a real person recognizes themselves or someone they know in it.

---

## UGC OUTPUT FORMAT (for reel-ugc)

When writing the UGC layer, deliver:

1. **The UGC angle** — one paragraph describing what this UGC looks like and why it fits the reference's DNA (not a template name, a specific description)

2. **The hook** — the exact first 3 seconds: what the viewer sees, what they hear, what text appears if any

3. **Shot list** — each moment in the UGC: what's filmed, from what angle, what's said (if anything), what's on screen

4. **What makes it feel real** — specific choices that prevent it from looking like produced UGC (the imperfection strategy)

5. **CapCut edit notes** — how to edit this UGC in CapCut to match the reference's pacing/style (references `knowledge/capcut-reference.md`)

---

## WHAT NEVER TO WRITE IN reel-ugc

- "Here's a classic UGC talking-head format..."
- "Try the honest review approach..."
- "Use the 'before and after' UGC structure..."
- Any suggestion that starts from a UGC template rather than from the reference breakdown

If the reference breakdown hasn't been read, do not write the UGC angle. Go read it first.
