## THE ALGORITHM (Pillar 0)

### How Distribution Works
Instagram tests every Reel with a **small batch of non-followers** first ("the audition").
- Strong early signals → wider waves of non-followers
- Weak signals → dies quietly
- **Follower count is almost irrelevant for Reels reach.** Accounts under 5K followers see ~20% view rates because each Reel is evaluated on its own merits.

### Signal Hierarchy (confirmed by Mosseri — highest to lowest weight)

| Rank | Signal | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **DM Shares (Sends Per Reach)** | Hardest to fake, strongest vote. 500 likes + 50 DM shares > 5K likes + 5 shares |
| 2 | **Watch Time & Retention** | Algorithm gates initial distribution here. Decision made in 1.7 seconds. Rewatch > completion |
| 3 | **Saves** | Signals long-term value. Educational content = 2.3x more saves than entertainment |
| 4 | **Comments (quality)** | NLP evaluates quality. Threaded conversations >> single emoji |
| 5 | **Likes** | Weakest signal. Vanity metric. Do not build strategy around likes |

**The question to ask before every Reel:** "Would someone send this to a specific person they know?"

### The Originality Score
Instagram fingerprints every Reel visually. Penalizes:
- Recycled clips (70%+ of another creator's visual/audio)
- TikTok/CapCut watermarks
- Low-effort reposts
- Aggregator-style reposting

Rule: **Every Reel must be native, clean, and original.** No watermarks. If repurposing from TikTok, re-record natively.

### Recommendation Eligibility (must pass ALL to enter non-follower pool)
- No watermarks from other platforms
- Must include audio (even ambient)
- Under 3 minutes
- Original content
- No community guideline violations

### The Trust Score
Every account has a rolling trust score. One bad post drags the next 4 down. **Quality consistency > posting frequency.** 3 strong Reels/week > 7 mediocre ones.

## SEO & CAPTIONS (Pillar 3)

### Caption as SEO
Instagram is a search engine. Google and Bing index Instagram captions.
- **Keywords first:** most important keywords in the first sentence
- Natural language — no keyword stuffing
- On-screen text also feeds the recommendation engine (dual purpose: mute viewers + algorithm)
- **Alt text** (Advanced Settings → Write Alt Text): hidden SEO weapon. Most creators skip it. Describe the visual content with a natural keyword.

### The Dwell Time Hack
Keep the first caption line as a cliffhanger so users tap "...more" to expand.
Once expanded, the Reel loops in the background → artificially doubles watch time.

**Triggers that work:**
- "The 4th tip is the one nobody talks about..."
- "I almost didn't post this because..."
- "Here's why this works even if you have zero experience..."

### Engineering DM Shares (Highest-Weight Signal)
Formats that drive DMs:
- **Relatable POV:** "POV: You're explaining your job to your family for the 100th time" → viewer thinks of someone, forwards it
- **Niche humor:** Broad humor gets likes. Specific humor gets sent
- **"Tag someone who..." moments:** frame it as a gift to forward
- **Useful checklists/frameworks:** "Send this to your friend who's starting a business"
- **High-arousal emotions:** Surprise, laughter, outrage, nostalgia, awe = sharing triggers

### Engineering Saves
- Bullet-point checklists
- Step-by-step frameworks with clear replicable steps
- Tool stacks and resource lists
- Data, statistics, proprietary insights
- Anything that looks like a reference someone returns to

### Engineering Comments (Micro-CTA)
Never ask for a like. Never use generic "Follow for more."

**What works:**
- Polarizing question: "Which works better — A or B?"
- Confession prompt: "Which of these 3 mistakes have you made?"
- Automation keyword: "Comment 'GROWTH' and I'll DM you the breakdown"

Real A/B test: "Follow for more" → 12 comments. "Which mistake have you made?" → 189 comments (1,475% increase).

### Hashtag Formula (2026)
Hashtags are filing labels, not discovery engines. Posts without hashtags now achieve 23% higher reach than hashtag-heavy posts. Real discovery = caption keywords + on-screen text.

**Formula:** 3–5 hashtags per Reel
- 1 broad category tag (50K–500K posts)
- 2 niche-specific tags
- 1–2 content-specific tags
- Never use tags with 10M+ posts (buried instantly)
- Change combinations every post
- Place at end of caption or first comment — never in first line

## GROWTH TACTICS (Pillar 5)

### Trial Reels (Most Underused Feature)
When you publish as a Trial Reel, Instagram shows it to non-followers ONLY before it hits your profile.
- If it performs well → push wider
- If it doesn't → disappears with no damage to your profile metrics

**Strategic uses:**
- Hook A/B testing: same content, two different opening lines → measure 3s hold rate
- Format testing: 15s vs 30s, talking head vs b-roll, trending vs original audio
- Niche expansion testing without confusing existing followers

**Only compare Trial Reels against other Trial Reels** — they underperform regular posts because they lack initial follower engagement.

### Trending Audio
- Audio with small upward arrow = currently rising
- Target tracks with **under 5,000–10,000 uses** (catch before saturation)
- TikTok trends migrate to Instagram 1–2 weeks later — monitor TikTok for early signals
- **Original audio gets algorithmic preference in 2026** — voiceovers, creator-made audio = effort signal
- Business accounts: verify audio availability before building content around it

### Niche Consistency
Instagram's "Your Algorithm" feature (Dec 2025) lets users toggle topic categories. Multi-niche accounts get diluted. **Define your lane tightly.** The algorithm needs to clearly categorize you to recommend you.

### Post-Publish Protocol
**First 30–60 minutes:** Stay active. Reply to every comment with 5+ words. Threaded conversations are a high-weight 2026 signal. Ignoring comments in the first hour kills this signal.

**Within 24–72 hours, check:**
- Completion rate → idea held attention
- Shares + saves → it mattered
- Non-follower reach → algorithm trusts it

## CREATIVE IGNITION SYSTEM (Pillar 7)

### The Fundamental Truth
People share content because it made them **feel something**. Sharing is emotional behavior, not analytical. High-arousal emotions drive shares: awe, excitement, amusement, surprise, anger, anxiety. Low-arousal emotions (contentment, sadness) rarely trigger shares.

### Why People Share (Hierarchy)
1. **Identity Signaling** — "This is who I am." Sharing reinforces the sharer's self-image. Ask: "What does sharing this say about the sharer?"
2. **Social Currency** — "I know something you don't." Inside tips, counterintuitive findings, "secret" information feel valuable to forward.
3. **Emotional Movement** — A shift from one emotion to another within the Reel. Tension → resolution. Anxiety → relief. Problem → unexpected solution. Multiple emotional peaks > single climax.
4. **Relational Targeting** — "This is so [specific person]." The narrower the scenario, the stronger the urge to send it. "Middle child at family dinners" outperforms "family struggles."
5. **Utility** — "They need to see this." Sharing as a gift. "Send this to your friend starting a business."

### Emotional Palette (by sharing power)
- **Awe & Inspiration** → extraordinary transformations, against-all-odds, before/after. Most-shared positive emotion.
- **Humor & Surprise** → unexpected twists, pattern-breaking punchlines. Strengthens relationships.
- **Outrage & Injustice** → controversial takes, calling out bad practices. Use carefully.
- **Nostalgia** → "Remember when..." Creates immediate bond.
- **Validation & Relief** → "I thought I was the only one." The "this is SO me" Reel that gets screenshotted instantly.

### Authenticity Imperative (2026)
AI can generate polished content instantly → polished content is now cheap. Raw, human content stands out.

- **Show the human.** Faces outperform faceless content. Natural speech > scripted delivery. Visible imperfections signal "this is real."
- **Lead with opinion, not just information.** "Here are 5 tips" = resource. "I've been doing this wrong for 2 years" = story. Stories get shared. Resources get saved. Best content does both.
- **The vulnerability hack:** "I almost didn't post this...", "Here's a mistake that cost me..." disarms the scroll reflex.
- **Creator-style beats brand-style.** Personal tone, first-person perspective, behind-the-scenes. People trust operators more than logos.

### Risk-Taking Framework
- **Be polarizing (within your values).** 80% love + 20% argue = outsized engagement. The argument IS content.
- **Break your own format.** If tightly edited content is plateauing → try one raw, unedited, straight-to-camera video. Novelty within audience expectations = pattern interrupt.
- **Post the "wrong" thing.** Use Trial Reels to test content that scares you. The Reel you almost didn't publish often breaks through because genuine vulnerability can't be manufactured.
- **Accept randomness.** Even perfect mechanics + perfect creative won't guarantee virality. Control the mechanics (floor) and creative (ceiling). The randomness is the weather.

### THE 2026 COUNTER-TREND WARNING
Hyper-edited content (jump cuts, kinetic text, zoom-ins) is now the **baseline cost of entry**, not a differentiator. Users experience dopamine fatigue from it. Slower, unedited, authentic "raw talk" videos now routinely outperform polished content because they feel more trustworthy.

**Variable format strategy:** Alternate between tightly edited Reels (educational/instructional) and raw authentic Reels (personal takes, confessions, opinions). If everyone in your niche zooms in 1.2x on frame one, the new pattern interrupt is standing still and speaking directly to camera.
